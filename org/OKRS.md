# OKRs — the one team's north star

The Director reads this every pass. **No objective here → no work.** Every task
the Director stamps `ready` must ladder up to one of these.

## Objective 1 — Ship a sellable v1
- **KR1** — core flow works end-to-end (target: demo-able by <date>)
- **KR2** — 5 design partners using it weekly

## Objective 2 — Be findable
- **KR1** — landing page live, explains the product in one screen
- **KR2** — 3 pieces of content published that a stranger would share

*This is a mirror of your real goal tree. Keep it honest — if the work doesn't
serve a line above, it's scope drift, not work.*
